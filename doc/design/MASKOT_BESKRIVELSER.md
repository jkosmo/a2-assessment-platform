# Maskot — beskrivelser til bildegenerering (ChatGPT)

Til produkteier, 2026-11-01. Åtte retninger for en maskot bygd på A-2-logoen, til å lime inn i ChatGPT.
Samme retninger er tegnet som enkel vektorgrafikk i skissen («Gamifisering: maskot og felles mål»), så
du kan sammenligne et rikt bilde med hvordan figuren blir i appen.

## Slik bruker du dem

1. Last opp A-2-logoen i samtalen (den runde, skifergrå med den hvite seil-A-en og «2»).
2. Lim inn **fellesstilen** under, én gang.
3. Lim inn **figurbeskrivelsen** for én retning, ordrett, og deretter **ark-bestillingen**. Det gir alle fire
   humør i ett bilde — det er den sikreste måten å få samme figur i alle fire.
4. Vil du se figuren i en situasjon (på månedsmøtet, med desserten), lim inn figurbeskrivelsen ordrett igjen
   og bytt bare ut det som står i klammene. **Endre aldri beskrivelsen** mellom bildene; det er den som
   holder figuren lik.

Beskrivelsene er på engelsk, fordi bildemodellene følger engelsk mer presist.

## Fellesstil (lim inn først)

> Style for every image in this conversation: friendly flat vector mascot illustration, clean bold shapes,
> thick rounded outlines in dark slate (#3E4656), limited palette: slate blue-grey #6E7B91 as the main
> colour, white #FFFFFF, warm cream background #F1EFEA, one accent colour brass #8A5F10, soft pink cheeks
> #F2A7A0. Simple and readable at 64 pixels. No text in the image unless I ask for it. The attached image
> is our company logo: a slate circle with a white letter A shaped like a sail and a small "2". The mascot
> must clearly be derived from this logo — reuse its circle, its sail-shaped A, and its "2".

## Ark-bestilling (lim inn etter figurbeskrivelsen)

> Make a character expression sheet of this mascot: one image, 2×2 grid, same character, same size and
> same angle in all four panels. Panel 1: full and happy, proud. Panel 2: a little hungry, hopeful, looking
> sideways. Panel 3: grumpy and offended, arms crossed if it has arms. Panel 4: dramatically starving,
> theatrical, one tear — funny, not sad. Keep every detail of the character identical across panels;
> only the expression and posture change.

## De åtte retningene (figurbeskrivelser)

### 1. Skipper — sirkelen blir en figur

> Mascot "Skipper": a round, plump creature whose body IS the slate-blue logo circle, slightly squashed
> like a soft ball. On top of its head grows a single white sail-shaped crest exactly like the sail-shaped
> letter A in the logo, which droops when it is sad and stands tall when it is proud. Two small white
> feet shaped like the number 2. Big round eyes with white highlights, small mouth, pink cheeks. No arms.

### 2. Skuta — seilet blir en båt

> Mascot "Skuta": a small, chubby sailboat with a face on its slate-blue hull: two round eyes and a small
> mouth near the waterline. Its single white sail is exactly the sail-shaped letter A from the logo, with
> the crossbar of the A as the boom. A tiny brass pennant at the top of the mast. It floats on two simple
> wavy lines. When it is full the sail is round with wind; when hungry the sail hangs slack.

### 3. A og 2 — et par

> Mascots "A og 2": a duo. "A" is a tall, slim character shaped like the white sail-shaped letter A from
> the logo, with thin legs, long arms and a calm, slightly serious face. "2" is a small, round, energetic
> sidekick shaped like the number 2, slate-blue, always half a step behind A and much more emotional.
> Both stand on a slate-blue circle like a little stage.

### 4. Seilmåka — en sjøfugl

> Mascot "Seilmåka": a round, cheerful seagull with a slate-blue body and a white head. One of its wings
> is shaped exactly like the sail-shaped letter A from the logo, and it shows off that wing. Short brass
> beak, round eyes, small orange feet. It looks like it would steal your lunch, but in a friendly way.

### 5. Kobben — en sel

> Mascot "Kobben": a round, chubby grey seal pup in the logo's slate-blue colour, lying like a soft ball.
> A white sail-shaped tuft on its head exactly like the sail-shaped letter A in the logo. Huge glossy dark
> eyes, whiskers, pink cheeks, small flippers. Very expressive: it begs for food like seals do.

### 6. Fyret — et fyrtårn

> Mascot "Fyret": a short, round lighthouse with a face on its slate-blue tower: two round eyes and a
> small mouth. White stripes on the tower, and its lantern room is shaped like the sail-shaped letter A
> from the logo. The light in the lantern glows brass-yellow when it is full and flickers weakly when it
> is hungry. Sits on a small rock.

### 7. Papirbåten — brettet av kurssider

> Mascot "Papirbåten": a small origami paper boat folded from white course pages with faint printed lines,
> the folded sail shaped like the sail-shaped letter A from the logo. A face on the folded hull: two round
> eyes, a small mouth. Sits in a puddle of slate-blue water. When hungry it is crumpled and soggy; when
> full it is crisp and freshly folded.

### 8. Dråpen — et rent abstrakt vesen

> Mascot "Dråpen": an abstract soft blob in the logo's slate-blue colour, like a drop of ink with a
> personality, with a white sail-shaped A as a highlight shape on its body. Big expressive eyes, tiny
> mouth, pink cheeks. Its shape stretches tall when happy and slumps flat into a puddle when hungry.

## Når en retning er valgt

Lim inn den valgte beskrivelsen ordrett og be om (a) vekststadier: «as an egg-like baby, a young one, and
a grown-up proud version», og (b) tilbehør: «with a tiny graduation cap», «with a small robot antenna».
Bildene brukes som forbilde; selve maskoten i appen tegnes som vektorgrafikk (SVG), så humør og bevegelse
kan styres i koden.
