The draft is never better than what you put in. Three sources are common.

### ![](asset:ikon-sakliste) Agenda

**Use when** the meeting followed a fixed plan.

- Gives the order of the minutes
- Shows which items were to be decided

### ![](asset:ikon-notater) Your own notes

**Use when** you took notes as the meeting went on.

- Capture decisions and deadlines
- Must be tidied before they are pasted in

### ![](asset:ikon-opptak) Recording

**Use when** the meeting was recorded with consent.

- Gives a word-for-word account
- Takes the longest to go through

## How to ask for the draft

Paste your notes in under this prompt:

```prompt
You are to write the minutes of a meeting.
Readers: [who will read them]
Form: decisions first, then action points with an owner and a deadline.
Use only what the notes below say. Write "unclear" where the notes say nothing.

[paste the notes]
```

You choose the language of the draft on the settings page, in the field marked in red:

![The settings page with the language field marked with a red frame](asset:img-innstillinger)

## What to include

| Part | Content | Taken from |
|---|---|---|
| Decisions | What was decided | Your own notes |
| Action points | Who does what, and when | Your own notes and the agenda |
| Present | Who took part | Agenda |
