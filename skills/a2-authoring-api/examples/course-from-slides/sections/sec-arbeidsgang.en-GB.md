Good minutes are made in four steps. Two come before you ask the AI for help, two after.

![Four steps from meeting to minutes, in the phases Before and After](asset:fig-arbeidsgang)

1. **Settle the purpose.** Who will read the minutes, and what will they use them for?
2. **Gather the notes.** Put the agenda, your own notes and any recording in one place.
3. **Write a draft.** Ask the AI for a draft based on the notes, in the form the readers need.
4. **Read and correct.** Check decisions, deadlines and names against the notes before you send.

> **Remember:** You answer for the minutes. The AI only writes the draft.

The notes from the board meeting say: *Present: Kari, Ola, Siri. Decision: budget approved. Ola sends
the budget to the auditor by 1 November. Not settled who books the venue.*

> **Draft from the AI:**
> - **Decision:** The budget was approved.
> - **Follow-up:** Ola sends the budget to the auditor by 15 November. Kari books the venue.
> - **Present:** Kari, Ola, Siri and Per.

:::skriv Find the errors in the draft before it is sent. Write what is wrong, and how you found it.
1. **The deadline is wrong:** the notes say 1 November, not 15. You find it when you check deadlines against the notes.
2. **Kari is given a task she has not been given:** the notes say it is not settled. The draft should have said «unclear».
3. **Per was not present:** he is not in the notes. You find it when you check names.

:::
