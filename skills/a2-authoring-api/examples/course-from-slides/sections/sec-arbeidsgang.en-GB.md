Good minutes are made in four steps. Two come before you ask the AI for help, two after.

![Four steps from meeting to minutes, in the phases Before and After](asset:fig-arbeidsgang)

1. **Settle the purpose.** Who will read the minutes, and what will they use them for?
2. **Gather the notes.** Put the agenda, your own notes and any recording in one place.
3. **Write a draft.** Ask the AI for a draft based on the notes, in the form the readers need.
4. **Read and correct.** Check decisions, deadlines and names against the notes before you send.

> **Remember:** You answer for the minutes. The AI only writes the draft.
